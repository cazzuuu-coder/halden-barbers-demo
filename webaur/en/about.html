ABOUT

Attention to the details that build trust.

WEBAUR is an independent web design studio. We help businesses improve their online presence with considered design, careful development and clear communication.

Design quality

A website should look like your business, not like a template. We pay attention to typography, spacing, photography and hierarchy so the first impression feels intentional.

Technical care

Design only works if the site is fast, readable and reliable on every screen. We build with clean front-end code, thoughtful performance and a structure that is easy to maintain.

Clear communication

The process is straightforward: discover, design, develop, launch. You will always know what is happening and what we need from you.

Tailored solutions

Every project is planned around the individual business. We do not sell a single layout with the logo swapped.

Attention to detail

Small things — a clear button, a readable menu on a phone, a form that is honest about what happens next — are what make a site feel considered. No invented awards, client logos or years in business. The work should speak for itself.
