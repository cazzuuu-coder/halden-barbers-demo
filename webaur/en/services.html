Frequently asked questions

What is included in each package?

Each package lists the pages and features that are included. If you need something that is not on the list, we will agree it separately before work begins.

Are domain and hosting included?

No. Domain registration and website hosting are not included in the design and development packages. We can advise you on simple, reliable options.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the scope and on how quickly we can review drafts together. We agree a timetable after the first conversation, once the work is clear — we do not quote a standard number of days in advance.

Is ongoing maintenance included?

No. Maintenance is a separate service. The build packages cover design, development and launch of the agreed site.

Can I use my own photographs and text?

Yes. If you already have photography or writing you would like to use, we will work with it. Additional photography or copywriting beyond the agreed scope is quoted separately.
