Work / Hale Barber Studio

Concept project

Hale Barber Studio

A five-page concept for an independent barbershop. The site is built around a quiet hour in the chair: bone, forest green, and photography of the room rather than stock smiles.

View live demo Back to work

The brief

Design a complete barbershop website that could stand as a commissioned piece: its own identity, several real pages, and a visit experience that does not invent bookings or reviews.

Objective

Show how WEBAUR would approach a small service business: typography, photography and a clear path, without a generic template look.

Audience

An independent barbershop that wants a site with the same care as the cut — small, specific, and calm rather than loud or promotional.

This is a concept project. It is not a paying-client website, and it does not invent customers, reviews or results.

Key features

  • Five pages: home, services, barbers, gallery, visit
  • A service menu with example prices
  • A staff portrait and a close gallery of the room
  • A visit page that does not pretend to take real bookings

The visit page explains how an appointment would work and states plainly that this is a concept. No form here claims to confirm a booking.

Design decisions

Let the type carry the brand.

Instrument Serif is used for the voice of the studio; Figtree keeps the body readable. The wordmark is typographic, so the identity can live in the layout rather than a separate mark.

Photograph the room, not a campaign.

The gallery and the hero are about chairs, light and tools. That feels closer to a real shop than a set of posed portraits.

Stay honest at the point of contact.

A live business would need a real booking tool. This concept stops short of pretending one exists.
