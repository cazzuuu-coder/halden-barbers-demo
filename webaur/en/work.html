WORK

Different businesses. Different digital experiences.

Three complete concept projects, each built as a multi-page brand site. They are independent design concepts — not paying-client work — made to show how WEBAUR would approach different businesses.

01 Concept project Barber

Hale Barber Studio

A five-page concept for an independent barbershop. The site is built around a quiet hour in the chair: bone, forest green, and photography of the room rather than stock smiles.

Design direction

Editorial and still. Instrument Serif for the voice, Figtree for the body, a bone field with forest-green bands. The layout leaves air around the work.

Main features

  • Five pages: home, services, barbers, gallery, visit
  • A service menu with example prices
  • A staff portrait and a close gallery of the room
  • A visit page that does not pretend to take real bookings

Design decisions

  • A typographic wordmark instead of a drawn logo
  • Photography of the room and the work, not lifestyle posing
  • A reservation path that stays honest about being a concept

02 Concept project Automotive detailing

Alto Detail

A showroom-like concept for a detailing studio: grey, oxide red, and cars photographed as objects in a bay. The site is five pages, including a results page built around before-and-after sliders.

Key feature

The same car, twice.

The sliders compare two treatments of the same photograph. They are illustrative, not proof of a paid job.

Design direction

Cinematic and industrial. Big Shoulders for the automotive voice, Source Sans 3 for the reading text, showroom grey with an oxide-red accent.

Main features

  • Five pages: home, services, results, process, contact
  • Three named packages with example prices
  • Interactive before-and-after sliders, honestly captioned
  • A process page that walks through a day in the bay

Design decisions

  • A typographic mark that reads like a manufacturer name
  • Comparisons that admit the “before” is a treated frame
  • Prices on the page, not hidden behind a quote form

03 Concept project Café

Maison Café

A four-page café concept in parchment, bottle green and copper. The site is built like a short journal: a table, a menu, a story, and a visit page that admits it is only in the story.

Design direction

Warm and typographic. Bodoni Moda for the display voice, Manrope for the body, a parchment ground with bottle-green bands and a copper accent.

Main features

  • Four pages: home, menu, story, visit
  • An editorial menu with example prices
  • A longer story page about the house
  • A visit page that does not invent a real door on a real street

Design decisions

  • A high-contrast serif as the café’s voice
  • Food photography used in sequences, not as a tiled catalogue
  • Hospitality copy that stays fictional and clearly labelled
