Concept project

Barber / Grooming

Barber Studio

A dark, premium concept for a grooming studio — built as a real page, labelled honestly as a study, not a client.

Design objective

Show how a small barbershop can feel considered and calm online, without relying on generic stock photography.

Target audience

Independent barbers and grooming studios that want a quiet, premium first impression on a phone and on a wide screen.

Design approach

A near-black ground, a display serif, a restrained gold accent and a short service list. Hours are present; no real address is invented. Booking is described as a connection to a tool the shop would already use.

Key features

  • Dark premium visual system
  • Clear service list and visit information
  • Mobile-first typographic layout
  • Space for a real booking integration later
