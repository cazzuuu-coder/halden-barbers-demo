Concept project

Automotive

Autohaus

A modern, technical and sophisticated concept for an automotive workshop.

Design objective

Give a workshop a site a mechanic would be willing to send — service data and a next step, not a brochure carousel.

Target audience

Independent garages and service workshops whose customers need hours, inspection, tyres and a way to request a slot.

Design approach

A light technical grid, monospaced labels and a four-step explanation of a visit. A signal red is used on actions and index numbers, not as a wash across the page.

Key features

  • Technical grid and service snapshot
  • Clear next-step call to action
  • Responsive layout for the workshop floor and the office
  • Room for a real enquiry or booking form
