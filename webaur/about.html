ABOUT

Attention to the details that build trust.

WEBAUR is an independent web design studio. We help businesses improve their online presence with considered design, careful development and clear communication.

Good design

A website should look like your business, not like a template. We pay attention to typography, spacing, photography and hierarchy so the first impression feels intentional.

Technical quality

Design only works if the site is fast, readable and reliable on every screen. We build with clean front-end code, thoughtful performance and a structure that is easy to maintain.

Clear communication

We keep the process straightforward: discover, design, build, launch. You will always know what is happening and what we need from you.

Helping businesses be found

We focus on making information easy to find and the next step obvious — a call, a visit, a message. No invented awards, client logos or years in business. The work should speak for itself.
