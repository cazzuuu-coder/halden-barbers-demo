Services

Everything your website needs.

Website design, development and maintenance — planned, built and handed over so your business can use the site with confidence.

Service 01

Website Design

Modern, tailored website designs that reflect your brand and make your business stand out. We start with your services, your customers and the impression you want to make, then set type, colour and layout so the site feels considered rather than templated.

You receive a clear visual direction, page layouts designed for a phone first, and a revision round agreed in the proposal. We do not promise unlimited design changes.

Service 02

Website Development

Fast, responsive and carefully built websites designed to work beautifully across all devices. We implement the design in clean HTML, CSS and JavaScript, test on common screen sizes, and launch on a domain and hosting arrangement that belongs to you.

This includes responsive design, essential or foundational SEO (titles, descriptions, headings, a sitemap and robots file), contact form integration that is actually connected and tested, and attention to website performance — compact images, tidy code and a light page weight. Booking tools or other integrations are included when they are appropriate and agreed in the quote. We do not promise guaranteed Google rankings or guaranteed sales.

Service 03

Website Maintenance

Reliable ongoing support to keep your website updated, secure and running smoothly. A care plan can cover availability, agreed content updates and coordination of hosting. The scope is written into the agreement so both sides know what is included — and what is billed separately.
