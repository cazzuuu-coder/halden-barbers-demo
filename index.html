Our story

A neighbourhood chair with old-world standards

Halden & Co. started as a single chair and a second-hand straight razor. Today we're a three-chair shop with the same idea at heart: unhurried appointments, honest advice and a cut that still looks good three weeks later.

We pair classic barbering techniques with modern styles — from tidy business cuts to sharp skin fades — and we never rush the finish. Coffee's on, the playlist is good and the conversation is optional.

  • Appointments, never assembly lines
  • Hot towel finish with every service
  • Locally made grooming products
